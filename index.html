How it works

From rally to coaching in three steps.

Set your phone behind the baseline and play your normal game. CourtSense does the rest.

01 · TRACK

It follows the shuttle and both players.

A red marker locks onto the shuttle and stays with it. Each player gets a name and a ring at their feet, on any court or in open ground.

02 · MEASURE

Every hit gets a shot, a speed and a rating.

Smash, clear, drop, drive, lift or net, forehand or backhand. Rally time, shot count and distance add up as you play.

03 · COACH

After the game, you know what to fix.

The coach picks the one or two changes that matter most, explains why, and gives you a drill and your own clips.

The science

Backed by sports science.

Studies show that seeing your own game, with clear numbers, helps you learn faster than being told what went wrong.

0.71 vs −0.09

Seeing beats being told

A review of 15 studies (853 learners) found visual feedback improved skill learning, while verbal feedback alone showed no effect.

Why CourtSense shows you, not just tells you.

Do I need a marked court?

No. CourtSense follows the players and the shuttle directly, so any court, hall or open ground works.

How accurate is the speed?

It is an estimate from one camera and is labelled that way. It works best with the phone on a stand and both players fully in view.
